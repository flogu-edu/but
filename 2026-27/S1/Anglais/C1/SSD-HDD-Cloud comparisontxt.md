HDD :
Its actually the default storage for massive storage at low cost. The technology stay on magnetic disk that rotate at high speed. A magnetic head set magnetic field of littles magnets on it at 0 or 1, physically. This technology allow us to store a lot of information on it for cheap (~25€/TB). But the physical deplacement of the head slow down the read and write speed. This storage method is not adapted for a OS disk who is permanently solicited for speed but it  is way more adapted for long term storage or archive.

SSD :
This technology appeared on the market for a few years from now. It is based on NAND storage wich use electricity to store information (like USB drive). There is no physical magnetic head on it, so the response time is way better than a HDD. With the years, the price go down enough to be competitive with HDD, but since 2024, the flash storage crisis (because of big tech AI corporations) has increased the price. Actually, the technology allow us to store the more speed demanding files like OS disk and every day files usage, but not massive storage anymore (~190€/TB for the slowest and cheapest items). 

Cloud :
This method of storage apparead for a few years from now with cloud services submitted by big tech company like Google (Google One and Cloud), Amazon (AWS), Microsoft (Azure), etc... Its popularity increased thanks to apps like google photo or drive with extremely cheap offers at the beginning. Now, the prices are less attractive because the economic plan was not viable. The principe is that we rent disk space on enterprises datacenters, then the entreprise store, manage backup. It is the safest method to store data, but the price is higher than local storage (we have to pay every months, ~5€/TB/month) and the speed depend on your internet speed and server speed. It is useful only for archive files or enterprise sensitives files (in the sense were the privacy is not important, because the entreprise can see absolutely all your data).
